A superflat compatible device made by the author with small contributions from various publicly archived components. It avoids hard items to get like ice. The schematics include both an assigned version and an unassigned one. Making your own itemlayout is highly recommended as it customizes storage to your own preferences. This storage has been used and tested in survival as well as in creative running thru the HC itemset
## Features
- Superflat compatible
- No hard items to get like ice
- 64 Bulk items and 32 categories capable of sorting 54 items each
- Bulk capacity: 220k
- Mis capacity: 15k
- Includes schematics with an assigned version and an unassigned one
- Highly recommended to make your own itemlayout to customize storage
